# Mutiny positioning truth (audit baseline, Oct 28 2026)

## Current positioning (homepage + /enterprise, Oct 2026)
- Homepage title: "The sales assistant built for customer-facing work". /enterprise H2: "The sales assistant that actually raises revenue per rep".
- Core story: notetaker on every call; learns how the team and each rep sells (voice, memory, playbooks); drafts follow-ups, meeting prep, CRM updates and customer-facing assets (pricing proposals, business cases, deal rooms, pitch decks, recaps, competitive comparisons, case studies) automatically after calls; "runs on triggers, not prompts"; team playbooks + personal AI; Mutiny MCP (use from Claude/Slack); replace legacy enablement tools.
- Enterprise proof: SOC 2 Type II, SSO/SAML, no model training on customer data, note-taking controls, RBAC, encryption.
- Buyers: Enterprise AEs, sales leaders (CRO/VP Sales), sales enablement. Marketing/ABM is secondary.

## AEO checklist (Matt's model)
- H1 phrased as the question buyers ask (or the exact category term)
- 40-60 word direct answer right under the H1 (TLDR)
- Fan-out H2s answering follow-up questions; FAQ block with concise answers
- Verifiable, attributed stats (customer name + metric + source)
- Fresh: visible updated date, current year, recent examples (~75% of AI citations are <30 days old)
- Comparison/"vs"/"alternatives" content within a topic; named competitors
- Internal links to the relevant product/use-case page; clean meta title + description
- Entity consistency: same one-line description of Mutiny everywhere
